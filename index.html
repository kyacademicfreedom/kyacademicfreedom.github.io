Open Letter to the University of Kentucky’s President

An open letter opposing the University’s proposed IT regulation. Anyone can sign or comment.

How to take part

  1. Sign or comment by Nov. 29 at 8 p.m. Eastern

    Use the signature and comment form. It matches the signature sections at the end of the letter. You can:

    • agree with the letter;
    • agree in part, or add context;
    • disagree fully or in part, or add context; or
    • sign anonymously, whether you agree or disagree, if you fear reprisal and want to say why.

  2. Send your own comment to the University

    Before Nov. 30, use the University’s feedback form for the IT regulation. The letter is not a substitute for your own comment.

  3. Please share this page

The regulation and the University’s response

  • The draft IT regulation (PDF) Draft dated Oct. 30, 2026, posted by the University.
  • The University’s public comment form Submit your own feedback on the draft directly to the University. Open through Nov. 30.
  • The University’s response (Nov. 16, 2026) Extended public comment by two weeks and said the draft “focuses on employees’ use of university technology and resources, not students or visitors.”
