Open Letter to the University of Kentucky’s President

An open letter opposing the University’s proposed IT regulation. Anyone can sign or comment. Signatures close Nov. 29, 2026, at 8 p.m. Eastern. The University’s public comment period closes Nov. 30.

How to take part

  1. Sign or comment by Nov. 29 at 8 p.m. Eastern using the signature and comment form. It matches the signature sections at the end of the letter. You can:
    • agree with the letter;
    • agree in part, or add context;
    • disagree fully or in part, or add context; or
    • sign anonymously, whether you agree or disagree, if you fear reprisal and want to say why.
  2. Send your own comment to the University before Nov. 30 using the University’s feedback form for the IT regulation. The letter is not a substitute for your own comment.
  3. Please share this page.
